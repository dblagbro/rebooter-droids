How adoption works

  1. Device boots without a token → POSTs /api/v1/device/announce with MAC + claims every ~5s while pending
  2. Hub records it; row appears here as pending
  3. You click Adopt (optionally setting a display name) → hub mints a fresh enrolment token, stashes it on the announcement
  4. Device's next announce poll (within ~5s while pending) returns the token; row flips to awaiting register
  5. Device uses the token against /api/v1/device/register → row flips to registered + a normal device row appears on /app/devices
