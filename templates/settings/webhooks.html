Outbound webhooks & notification channels

The hub can call out to a chat tool or a custom endpoint when something happens — a watchdog rule fired, a device went offline, a firmware deployment finished. Every outbound request is SSRF-guarded (private / loopback / link-local / CGNAT targets are rejected and the connection is pinned to the validated IP) and queued — a slow receiver can never affect the hub.
